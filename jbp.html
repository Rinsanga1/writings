Writings Reading List JBP

JBP

Books authored and recommended by Jordan B. Peterson, compiled from his public reading list.

The list mixes his own three books with a longer set of recommendations grouped by domain. Peterson treats this less as a syllabus than as a map of the territory he draws on across his lectures and writing:

  • Peterson's own work: Maps of Meaning, 12 Rules for Life, Beyond Order, We Who Wrestle with God
  • Recommendations: ~115 books across literature, clinical psychology, neuroscience, religion, history, and global development

Suggested Approaches to Reading

  • By Author Cluster: Peterson returns again and again to Dostoevsky, Nietzsche, Jung, Eliade, and Solzhenitsyn — reading multiple works by one author compounds quickly.
  • Thematic: Pair literature with the psychology that interprets it (e.g. Crime and Punishment with Jung's Aion, or Solzhenitsyn with Frankl's Man's Search for Meaning).
  • Domain-First: Pick one category and work through it — the religion and clinical lists are tightly curated and reward sequential reading.

Tips for Getting Started

  1. Begin with Dostoevsky (Crime and Punishment) and Solzhenitsyn (The Gulag Archipelago, abridged) — they anchor the moral arc of the whole list.
  2. Pair fiction with one psychology title at a time to avoid getting stuck in a single register.
  3. The Jung volumes reward order: Modern Man in Search of a Soul → Archetypes → Aion → Mysterium Coniunctionis.
  4. Source list: jordanbpeterson.com/books.
